# AI Policy

- **All AI usage must be disclosed,** including the extent of usage and which tool was used.
- **You should understand the entire change and are responsible for the code and all actions taken.** Take time and care.
- **PR descriptions and comments must not be LLM generated.** Any PR with obviously LLM written text will be immediately closed without review as it shows a lack of care, effort and understanding. This applies especially with multiple PRs in a short timeframe. If fixed the PR may be reopened.
- Above applies to issues too but is less strictly enforced.
- There is no vouch/etc system at this time but one may be added in the future.

This policy is in place primarily to stop low-effort/slop PRs, not due to an anti-AI stance. Porffor's code intentionally has a higher bar than typical projects to maintain quality/size/etc. If your issue/PR/etc is closed and this is linked, please understand and do not be offended :)
